About migovt.org

What this site is, what it isn't, and why it's built the way it is.

migovt.org is not an official government website. It's a simulated government interface and does not represent any real government body or authority. Read the full disclaimer.

What this site is

migovt.org is a simulated interface for a Michigan state and local government portal. It's built to look and behave like a real government website — with the departments, agencies, forms, and tools you'd expect to find on one — but the site itself is not affiliated with, operated by, or endorsed by the State of Michigan, Genesee County, the City of Flint, or any other government entity.

Think of it as a working model: a fully fleshed-out example of how a government's various branches, departments, and services might be organized and presented online, built for demonstration rather than official use.

What's built out, and what's referenced

Not everything linked from this site leads to fully built-out content — some pages exist in depth, and others are referenced the way a real government site would link to a department without every visitor needing to visit it.

  • Built out

    Pages like the state and local government directories, housing, voting, employment, and the visitor section are fully built with real structure, descriptions, and — where noted — live tools.

  • Referenced

    Some departments, offices, and pages are named and linked throughout the site as part of the larger structure, even where a dedicated page hasn't been built out yet.

Genesee County & the City of Flint

Where this site needed a real-feeling example of local government, it uses Genesee County and the City of Flint — real places in Michigan. That choice is for the sake of a concrete, consistent example throughout the site, not a claim that this site represents their actual governments in any way.

Who maintains it

Within the site's own fiction, migovt.org is presented as maintained by a Department of Technology — the entity credited in the footer of every page. Its role here is purely to give the simulated site a consistent, plausible owner, in keeping with how a real government's central IT or digital services office might run a state web portal.

In short: migovt.org is not a real government website, and nothing on it should be used to conduct actual government business, verify your rights, or make legal or civic decisions.

For the complete disclaimer, see migovt.org/disclaimer.
