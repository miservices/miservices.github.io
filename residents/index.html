Good to know

More for residents

A few other things that come up often for people living in Michigan.

New to Michigan

If you've just moved here, a few things tend to come up early: registering your vehicle, getting a Michigan driver's license or state ID, and registering to vote at your new address.

Deadlines that matter

Two dates residents plan around every year: the state income tax filing deadline in the spring, and voter registration deadlines ahead of each election.

Family & community support

Beyond housing and jobs, the state runs assistance programs covering food, childcare, healthcare, and support for seniors and people with disabilities — all reachable through Services.

Local matters, too

A lot of day-to-day life as a resident — property records, local courts, city services — runs through your county and city government rather than the state directly.
