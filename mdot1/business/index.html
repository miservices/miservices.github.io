Doing business with MDOT.

Information for contractors, vendors, and small businesses working on Michigan's roads, bridges, and transit projects — including opportunities right here in Flint.

01

Bidding & contracts

MDOT regularly lets construction and maintenance contracts for road, bridge, and transit projects across the state. Contractors can review upcoming lettings, download plans and specifications, and submit bids through our electronic bidding system.

  1. 1

    Get prequalified

    Register and prequalify with MDOT before bidding on construction contracts.

  2. 2

    Review upcoming lettings

    Browse posted projects, including scope, location, and estimated timelines.

  3. 3

    Download plans & specifications

    Access project plans, special provisions, and estimates for projects you're bidding on.

  4. 4

    Submit your bid

    Bids are submitted electronically ahead of the posted letting date.

02

Vendor registration

Suppliers, consultants, and service providers can register as MDOT vendors to be considered for contracts outside of construction lettings — including engineering services, equipment, and materials.

Who should register

Suppliers, consultants, engineering firms, and service providers looking to work with MDOT.

What you'll need

Business registration details, relevant licenses, and insurance documentation.

03

Small business & DBE program

MDOT's Disadvantaged Business Enterprise (DBE) program works to ensure small and disadvantaged businesses have a fair opportunity to compete for state and federally funded transportation contracts.

  • Certified DBE firms can be listed in MDOT's directory, connecting them with prime contractors seeking subcontractors.
  • MDOT sets participation goals on federally funded projects to support DBE inclusion.
  • Support and outreach programs help small businesses build capacity to compete for larger contracts.

Subcontracting opportunities

Prime contractors on MDOT projects often need subcontractors for specialty work — check the DBE directory or reach out to prime contractors directly on active lettings.

04

Permits & licensing

Businesses hauling oversize or overweight loads, working near state right-of-way, or connecting a driveway or utility to a state road need the appropriate MDOT permit before starting work.
