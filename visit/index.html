Good to know

A few other things

Road conditions can change quickly during Michigan winters, so it's worth checking travel information before any long drive. Most state parks require a vehicle entry permit, available at the park or ahead of time. And if you find yourself thinking about staying longer than a visit, Michigan also offers state citizenship to people who want to formally become residents.
