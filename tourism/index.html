Genesee County & Flint

Right where you're starting

A few well-known stops in and around Flint before you head further out.

Arts & Culture

Flint Cultural Center

A campus of museums, a planetarium, and performance venues in the heart of Flint — a good stop for art, science, and live shows all in one area.

Historic Village

Crossroads Village & Huckleberry Railroad

A recreated 19th-century village with a scenic train ride running through the grounds, popular with families and seasonal events throughout the year.

Historic Estate

Applewood Estate

A historic estate and gardens open for tours, offering a look at the area's history alongside grounds worth a slow walk.

Local Market

Flint Farmers' Market

A year-round indoor market with local vendors, food, and produce — a reliable stop whether you're passing through or staying a while.
