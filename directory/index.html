State Government

Directory of State Government

A structural directory of Michigan's state government — the executive, legislative, and judicial branches, along with the departments, bureaus, divisions, and units that make each of them run.

Branch

Executive Branch

Carries out and enforces state law. Led by the Governor, it includes the statewide elected offices, the principal departments, and the public safety and licensing agencies that handle the day-to-day work of state government.

Governor

The state's chief executive, elected statewide to a four-year term. Signs or vetoes legislation, appoints department heads and judges to fill vacancies, prepares the annual budget proposal, and commands the National Guard.

Lt. Governor

Elected on the same ticket as the Governor. Presides over the Senate, casting a vote only to break a tie, steps in as acting Governor when needed, and typically leads special initiatives assigned by the Governor.

Attorney General

The state's chief legal officer. Represents Michigan in litigation, issues formal legal opinions to state agencies, and oversees the bureaus responsible for major criminal investigations and correctional legal matters.

Bureau of Criminal Investigation

Investigates complex and multi-jurisdictional crimes on behalf of the Attorney General's office, working alongside county prosecutors and local police.

Major Crimes Division

Investigates homicides, violent crime, and organized criminal activity that cross county lines or require statewide coordination.

Special Investigations Division

Handles financial crime, fraud, and cyber-enabled offenses, including cases involving state contracts and benefit programs.

Public Integrity Division

Investigates corruption, bribery, and misconduct allegations involving public officials and government employees.

Bureau of Correctional Administration

Provides legal review and administrative oversight on matters involving the state's correctional system, including parole eligibility disputes and inmate litigation, in coordination with the Department of Corrections.

Secretary of State

Serves as Michigan's chief elections officer and record-keeper. Oversees driver and vehicle records, professional and business licensing, and the administration of state elections.

Bureau of Licensing and Regulatory Affairs

Issues and regulates the licenses that let people drive, practice a profession, and operate a business in the state.

Division of Professional Licensing

Licenses and credentials professionals such as contractors, cosmetologists, and other regulated trades.

Division of Driver Licensing and Records

Issues driver's licenses and state ID cards and maintains driving records and history.

Division of Business and Occupational Licensing

Registers businesses and issues occupational licenses required to legally operate in the state.

Division of Licensing Enforcement and Compliance

Investigates licensing violations and enforces compliance with state licensing requirements.

Bureau of Elections

Administers state elections, maintains voter registration rolls, and oversees campaign finance disclosure requirements.

State Police

Michigan's statewide law enforcement agency, providing patrol, investigative, and specialized public safety services across the state.

Field Services Bureau

Oversees uniformed patrol, highway enforcement, and specialized tactical operations conducted in the field.

Field Operations Division

Troopers who patrol state highways and communities, respond to calls for service, and enforce traffic law.

Commercial Vehicle Enforcement Division

Inspects commercial trucks and buses and enforces weight, safety, and hazardous-materials regulations.

Special Operations Division

Houses the department's specialized tactical and emergency-response teams, deployed for high-risk or unusual incidents.

Emergency Support Team

A tactical response and crisis-negotiation team deployed for high-risk incidents such as barricaded suspects and hostage situations.

Aviation Unit

Operates helicopters and fixed-wing aircraft in support of search and rescue, pursuits, and aerial surveillance.

Bomb Squad

Handles explosive-ordnance disposal, suspicious-package response, and post-blast investigations.

Marine Services Team

Patrols the state's lakes and waterways, enforcing boating safety laws and assisting in water rescues.

Tactical Bike Team

Provides bicycle-mounted patrol for crowded events, campuses, and dense urban areas.

Investigations and Intelligence Division

Coordinates major criminal investigations and the sharing of intelligence across local, state, and federal law enforcement.

Criminal Investigations Unit

Investigates major crimes that require specialized expertise or coordination across multiple jurisdictions.

Michigan Intelligence Operations Center

A fusion center that gathers, analyzes, and shares criminal and public-safety intelligence with partner agencies.

Administrative Services Bureau

Manages the internal operations of the department, including oversight of personnel conduct and the recruitment and training of new troopers.

Transparency and Accountability Division

Reviews internal conduct matters and provides legal support to the department.

Professional Standards Section

Investigates complaints and allegations of misconduct involving department personnel.

Legal Resources Section

Provides legal counsel and guidance to department leadership and personnel.

Recruitment and Training Division

Recruits new trooper candidates and oversees their training from the academy through field certification.

Recruitment Section

Recruits and screens candidates for trooper and civilian positions within the department.

Training & Academy Section

Runs the training academy and ongoing certification programs for sworn personnel.

National Guard

The state's military force, under the command of the Governor. Responds to natural disasters and state emergencies and can also be called into federal service.

Headquarters

The central command overseeing all components of the Michigan National Guard.

Office of the Adjutant General

The senior officer who commands the Michigan National Guard on behalf of the Governor.

Office of the Inspector General

Conducts inspections and investigations to ensure the Guard operates within regulation and policy.

Special Forces Group

A specialized unit trained for high-readiness missions beyond standard infantry operations.

117th Military Police Brigade

Provides law enforcement, security, and force-protection support across both the Army and Air components of the Guard.

Army Guard

The land-based component of the Michigan National Guard.

125th Infantry Battalion

An infantry unit trained for ground combat operations and state emergency response.

Air Guard

The aviation component of the Michigan National Guard.

127th Wing

A flying wing responsible for aircraft operations, air support, and related missions.

Department of Treasury

Manages state finances, including tax collection, the state budget, and management of public funds and investments.

Department of Corrections

Operates the state's prison system and oversees parole and probation supervision for people under state correctional supervision.

Department of Transportation

Plans, builds, and maintains the state's highways, roads, bridges, and public transit infrastructure.

Department of Natural Resources

Manages state parks, forests, and wildlife, and regulates hunting, fishing, and outdoor recreation on state land.

Michigan Capitol Police

Provides security and law enforcement services for the State Capitol complex and its visitors, staff, and elected officials.

Michigan Commission on Law Enforcement Standards

Sets statewide training and certification standards that police officers must meet to serve in Michigan.

Branch

Legislative Branch

Writes and enacts state law.

Legislature

The state's lawmaking body. Unlike most states, Michigan's Legislature is unicameral — a single chamber of elected representatives who draft, debate, and pass bills, and approve the state budget before it goes to the Governor.

Branch

Judicial Branch

Interprets state law and resolves legal disputes.

Michigan Judiciary

The state's court system. Michigan keeps this simple with just two levels of courts: District Courts, where cases are tried, and the Supreme Court, which hears appeals and has the final word on the law.

Supreme Court

The state's highest court, hearing appeals on the most significant legal and constitutional questions and setting precedent for all lower courts.

Clerk of the Supreme Court

Maintains the Court's official records, filings, and case dockets.

Supreme Court Marshal

Oversees courtroom security and operations for the Supreme Court.

State Court Administrative Office

Provides administrative support and oversight to trial courts throughout the state.

State Bar of Michigan

The professional association responsible for licensing attorneys and setting standards of conduct for the legal profession in the state.

District Courts

Michigan's only trial-level court. Rather than splitting cases across separate civil, criminal, family, or traffic courts, the District Courts hear the full range of trials — civil disputes, criminal cases, family matters, probate, and traffic — before an appeal would go to the Supreme Court.
