How hiring works

A few things worth knowing before you apply.

Equal opportunity

State, county, and city government hire without regard to race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or any other legally protected status.

How to apply

Each listing links to its own application. Expand a listing to review the full description and requirements before you apply.

Closing dates

Postings close on the date listed — some positions, especially field and seasonal roles, can close earlier if enough applications come in.
